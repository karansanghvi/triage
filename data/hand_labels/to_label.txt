Design Doc: Order History Service — Storage Decision
Status: Accepted (Sep 22)

Context. The new Order History Service stores every order, its line items, refunds, and status changes, and serves the "My Orders" page and the support console. We expect ~40M orders in year one.

Options considered. (1) PostgreSQL 16 on our managed database platform. (2) MongoDB Atlas.

Discussion. MongoDB's flexible documents are a natural fit for orders with varying line-item shapes, and the team has some experience with it from the catalog service. However, refunds and status changes must update several records atomically, and finance reporting relies on joins between orders, refunds, and payouts. Cost was roughly equal at our projected size.

Decision. PostgreSQL 16, with line-item attributes in a JSONB column.
